ABOUT

NEON DASH: CYBER RUNNER is a cyberpunk pixel art endless runner built with Love2D. Play as BYTE, a rogue AI subroutine escaping AXIOM Corporation's mainframe. Jump, slide, and dodge obstacles as you race against deletion.

Engine: Love2D 11.4 (Lua)
Web Runtime: love.js (Emscripten)
Resolution: 960×600 (2× scale from native 480×300)
Frame Rate: 60 FPS
